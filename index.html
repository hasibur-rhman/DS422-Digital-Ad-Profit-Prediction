Viva Defense Strategy

Data Leakage Prevention & Feature Architecture

Strictly isolating pre-launch features from post-campaign outcomes to ensure honest, real-world generalization.

Category 1 • Direct Math Identity

revenue & ad_spend

Since profit = revenue - ad_spend, including these features creates an artificial mathematical identity resulting in a fake R² = 1.000 with zero real predictive value.

Viva Answer: "Excluding mathematical components prevents the model from trivially memorizing the definition of profit."
Category 2 • Post-Campaign Outcomes

conversions, clicks, actual_cpc

These metrics can only be observed after the campaign has already run. Furthermore, actual_cpc was audited as a 100.0% exact duplicate of the derived CPC metric.

Viva Answer: "True decision support models only utilize features available at the moment the media plan is approved."
Category 3 • Derived Marketing KPIs

CTR, CPC, conversion_rate, CPA, ROAS

Calculated post-hoc ratios (revenue / ad_spend). Along with campaign_id (random hash) and raw start_date, all were cleanly dropped.

Final Feature Count: 28 Actionable Pre-Launch Predictors (9 Continuous + 19 Categorical).
Zero Multicollinearity Verified

Variance Inflation Factor (VIF) Audit: All 9 Predictors < 1.0003

Audited using pure OLS regression (VIF = 1 / (1 - R²)). All continuous features have negligible collinearity, proving high feature independence.

Critical Viva Question

Why did Decision Tree fail with negative R² (-0.1572)?

Single Decision Trees split space into rigid orthogonal step functions. When target profit has extreme positive skewness (13.03) and high variance, test samples fall into leaf bins with wildly unrepresentative means, making variance worse than predicting the horizontal mean line.

Tuning Impact

Why did Tuned XGBoost achieve best generalization?

RandomizedSearchCV restricted tree depth to 4 and applied L1/L2 penalties (reg_alpha=1.0, reg_lambda=3.0). This mitigated leaf overfitting and reduced test error by -$1,905.12.
