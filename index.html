Critical Domain Rule & Viva Defense

Why 13 Columns Were Strictly Excluded

Preventing synthetic over-optimism and ensuring true pre-launch decision-making utility.

Category 1: Math Identity

revenue & ad_spend

Since profit = revenue - ad_spend, feeding these columns into regression creates a trivial mathematical identity resulting in a fake R² = 1.000 with zero real-world deployment value.

Viva Justification: "Pre-launch models cannot know final revenue or spend; using them guarantees data leakage."
Category 2: Post-Campaign Outcomes

clicks, conversions, actual_cpc

These metrics can only be observed after an ad campaign has already concluded. Furthermore, actual_cpc was audited as a 100.0% exact duplicate of the derived CPC metric.

Viva Justification: "Actionable media planning requires forecasting profit before impression delivery begins."
Category 3: Derived Marketing KPIs

CTR, CPC, conversion_rate, CPA, ROAS

Ratios derived post-hoc from spend/revenue. Along with high-cardinality campaign_id (random hash) and unparsed start_date, all were dropped.

Final Matrix: 28 Pre-Launch Predictors (9 Continuous + 19 Categorical) yielding 78 One-Hot Features.
Multicollinearity Audit

Variance Inflation Factor (VIF) < 1.0003 for All 9 Predictors

Audited via OLS regression (VIF = 1 / (1 - R²)). All features scored between 1.0001 and 1.0003, well below the 5.0 threshold, confirming complete feature independence.

Viva Defense Question 1

Why did Decision Tree fail with negative R² (-0.1572)?

Single Decision Trees partition space via orthogonal step boundaries. On a target with extreme positive skewness (13.03) and heavy viral outliers, test samples fall into leaf nodes with wildly unrepresentative means, yielding residual sum of squares worse than predicting the horizontal mean line.

Viva Defense Question 2

Why did Tuned XGBoost lead the benchmark?

RandomizedSearchCV restricted tree depth to 4 and injected L1/L2 shrinkage penalties (reg_alpha=1.0, reg_lambda=3.0). This smoothed leaf boundaries and cut test RMSE by -$1,905.12.

Technical Implementation

Google Colab T4 GPU & Disconnect Recovery

Zero risk of lost progress via persistent Google Drive checkpoints.

01

Dual-Environment Detection

Automatically detects Colab (google.colab in sys.modules) vs local runtime. Automatically mounts Drive to /content/drive/MyDrive/Lab_EVAL_E1 and archives missing datasets.

02

Sub-10s Disconnect Resume

All 6 models and tuned booster are checkpointed as .joblib and native .json. Reconnecting and clicking Runtime → Run All restores state in under 10 seconds.

03

Tesla T4 GPU Acceleration

Leverages CUDA GPU acceleration via tree_method="hist", device="cuda" for XGBoost, with multi-core parallelization (n_jobs=-1) for Scikit-learn regressors.
